TEST ON A REAL IPHONE BEFORE YOU TRUST THIS
============================================

I could not test this on real iOS hardware - no Safari/iPhone available in
my environment. Everything was verified by simulation. Please run this on an
actual iPhone once it's deployed, on real cellular data if you can, not just
your home wifi:

1. Open the Netlify link on the iPhone. The home page should appear within
   a couple of seconds. If it hangs or the tab goes white/reloads itself,
   that is iOS's out-of-memory tab kill - screenshot it and send it to me.

2. Tap "Open BookFlip" on the general MBA Newsletter. It should open within
   a few seconds and show page 1.

3. Swipe/flip through at least 15 pages. Pages should render sharp, not stay
   blank. If a page stays permanently blank, note the page number.

4. Rotate the phone (portrait <-> landscape) two or three times while reading.
   The book should resize cleanly with no missing or doubled pages.

5. Tap the fullscreen icon (the corners icon).
     - iOS 17.4 or newer: should expand to fullscreen.
     - Older iOS: the button should not be there at all (this is intentional -
       see READ-ME-FIRST.txt). If you see the button but tapping it does
       nothing, tell me your iOS version.

6. Tap the download icon (down arrow).
     - Should open the PDF in a new Safari tab, showing Safari's own PDF
       viewer with a Share icon top-right. Tap Share -> Save to Files to
       actually save it - that's the correct iOS flow, not a bug.

7. Go to Settings -> Safari -> Advanced -> Website Data on the phone
   afterwards, find the site, and check it isn't holding an unreasonable
   amount of storage after just reading a few pages.

Anything that doesn't match the above, tell me exactly what step and what
you saw (a screenshot is ideal) and I'll dig into that specific failure
instead of guessing again.
